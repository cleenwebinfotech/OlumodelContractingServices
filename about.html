Who We Are

Olumodel Contracting Services

We are a property care and professional services company based in Lower Sackville, Nova Scotia. We are committed to providing dependable, professional, and affordable solutions for your home, business, and property maintenance needs.

From snow removal and lawn care to professional painting, cleaning, moving truck rentals, automobile detailing, and glass railing installations, we offer a wide range of services designed to make your life easier and keep your property looking its best.

Whether you are a homeowner, property manager, landlord, or business owner, you can count on us for quality workmanship, attention to detail, and excellent customer service.

Our Mission

Making Property Care Easier

At Olumodel Contracting Services, our mission is to make property care, maintenance, and improvement easier for individuals and businesses. We believe every property deserves professional attention, quality service, and reliable care.
